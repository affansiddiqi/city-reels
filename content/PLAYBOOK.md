# Playbook — city × persona

## The system

1. Pick a country's biggest city (or its real elite hub — see the note below).
2. Attack it one persona at a time.
3. Each reel targets exactly one group, with tells only an insider would know.
4. Exhaust the city, then move to the next.

The specificity is the product. A reel about "Baku" reaches nobody. A reel about
**Baku IT girls** reaches every Baku IT girl, and each one sends it to four more.

**Done so far:** Baku — rich spouse, rich teens. Islamabad — rich spouse (written,
unposted).

---

## The rule that beats "biggest city"

Biggest ≠ best. Target the city where the **class structure is most visible and
most argued about**. Sometimes that's the capital, not the population leader:

| Country | Biggest | Better target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pakistan | Karachi | **Islamabad / Lahore** | Sharper, more legible elite; Lahore has the old-money layer |
| India | Delhi | **Mumbai / Delhi** | Both work; Mumbai = money, Delhi = power |
| Turkey | Istanbul | **Istanbul** | Both in one city (Nişantaşı vs the rest) |
| Morocco | Casablanca | **Casablanca / Rabat** | Casa = money, Rabat = state |
| Nigeria | Lagos | **Lagos** | Lekki/Ikoyi vs mainland is the whole joke |
| Vietnam | Ho Chi Minh | **Ho Chi Minh** | District 1 vs everywhere else |

Signals that a city will work: a named rich neighbourhood everyone recognises, a
school hierarchy people can rank from memory, a status car, and a big diaspora
that engages with hometown content.

## The formula that makes these work

Every strong reel in this format is the same shape:

1. **A title that names the target.** "How to spot a Baku IT girl." The person
   has to know in one second that this is about them.
2. **A run of specific tells**, each verifiable by an insider — a place, a brand,
   a phrase, a price, an institution. Generic beats get scrolled.
3. **At least one tell that stings.** Affectionate, not cruel. It's the one
   people screenshot.
4. **A closing "sealed deal" line** — the most extreme version of the tell.

Never fewer than six specific proper nouns per reel. That's the difference
between "relatable" and "how do you know my life."

## Naming convention

Content files: `content/<city>_<persona>.json`
IDs inside: `<city>-<persona>-NN`

```
content/baku_it_girls.json      →  baku-it-girls-01 … 05
content/lahore_founders.json    →  lahore-founders-01 … 05
```

Keeps `videos/` and `queue.json` readable at a glance once there are hundreds.

---

## Sequencing

Run one persona to exhaustion (4–6 tips) before switching. The series format is
what makes people follow — a single reel gets a view, a series gets a follower.
Post the whole persona, then move to the next one in the same city. Only move
cities once the current one is mined out.
